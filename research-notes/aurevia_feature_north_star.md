## Purpose
This document serves as a **north star reference** for:

---

## 1. Core Positioning

Aurevia is an **AI sales co-pilot for ecommerce (Shopify-first)** designed to:

- Increase conversion rate
- Increase average order value (AOV)
- Reduce support load
- Guide shoppers from intent → discovery → decision → checkout

### Key framing:
- Not a support chatbot
- Not a generic AI assistant
- A **conversion-focused, consultative sales agent**

---

## 2. Core Differentiation (North Star)

### 2.1 Consultative Commerce
- Asks discovery questions before recommending
- Understands intent, not just keywords
- Reduces decision fatigue

### 2.2 Commerce Execution Layer
- Add to cart directly from chat
- Modify cart (update/remove)
- Generate checkout links
- Drive purchase completion

### 2.3 Revenue Optimization
- Contextual upsells (not spammy)
- Bundle recommendations
- Cart recovery in-session
- Guided checkout nudges

### 2.4 Unified Sales + Support
- Handles FAQs, policies, shipping queries
- Prevents drop-offs caused by uncertainty
- Reduces support tickets

### 2.5 Merchant-Controlled AI
- Grounded in catalog + knowledge base
- No hallucinated products
- Custom tone and rules
- Human escalation built-in

## 8. Competitive Comparison Lens

Use this checklist when comparing competitors:

### Discovery Quality
- Does it ask questions before recommending?
- Or does it just return search results?

### Recommendation Quality
- Context-aware vs generic suggestions
- Justification provided vs none

### Commerce Actions
- Can it add to cart?
- Can it generate checkout links?

### Upsell Intelligence
- Contextual vs spammy
- Triggered at the right moment?

### Support Coverage
- Handles real questions or deflects?
- Reduces support load?

### Analytics
- Revenue impact tracked?
- Or just chat metrics?

### Merchant Control
- Can merchants shape behavior?
- Or is it black-box AI?
